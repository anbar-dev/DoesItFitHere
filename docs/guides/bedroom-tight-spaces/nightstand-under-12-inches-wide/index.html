Quick verdict

For a nightstand under 12 inches wide, the safest choices are open-shelf narrow tables, C-shaped side tables, or wall-mounted bedside shelves. A drawer can be useful, but only if you have enough side clearance to pull it open.

Measure this before buying

  • Gap from mattress edge to wall, wardrobe, radiator, or walkway.
  • Bed height from floor to mattress top, so the tabletop is usable.
  • Outlet and plug depth behind or beside the table.
  • Drawer swing or shelf access after bedding hangs over the edge.

Common fit mistakes

  • Measuring the gap before bedding hangs over the side.
  • Blocking the only reachable outlet.
  • Buying a drawer table with no drawer clearance.
  • Choosing a tabletop far below or above mattress height.

FAQ

What can I use instead of a nightstand in a tiny bedroom?

A C-table, wall shelf, clip-on bedside shelf, or narrow open table can replace a standard nightstand.

How wide should a small nightstand be?

For tight spaces, search by your maximum measured width and leave a little side clearance so the table is usable.

Should a nightstand be level with the mattress?

It is usually easier to use when the tabletop is close to mattress height, especially for a phone, lamp, or water glass.
