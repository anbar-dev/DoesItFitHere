About

Buying advice for spaces that do not forgive guesses.

Fits In My Space is a practical library of shopping guides for narrow, shallow, low-clearance, under-sink, over-door, and awkward spaces.

The angle

Most organization content starts with pretty bins. This site starts with the measurement that can ruin the purchase: width, depth, height, lid swing, drawer pull, pipe position, outlet clearance, baseboard depth, or walkway space.

The page standard

Each finished guide should explain what to measure, which product types usually fit, what to skip, and the common clearance mistakes that cause returns.

Editorial rules

What makes a guide worth publishing.

The site is built for shoppers who already have a physical constraint and want to avoid buying the wrong size.

It starts with a measurement

A guide should name the limiting dimension clearly: under 10 inches wide, low ceiling, shallow cabinet, pipe gap, or under-bed clearance.

It separates fits from maybes

The `fits if / skip if` table matters because many products look close enough until a lid, drawer, hinge, or caster needs room.

It includes skip notes

Useful buying advice should tell you when not to buy: too deep, too tall, too wide, blocked by pipes, blocked by doors, or impossible to access.

How product links are handled

Some pages include Amazon affiliate links. If you click one and buy something, the site may earn a commission. The goal is still to make the guide useful before the link: product picks should follow from the fit problem and the measurement checklist.
