Good guide requests

  • A concrete space: under sink, beside fridge, behind door, under bed, narrow hallway.
  • The limiting measurement: width, depth, height, clearance, or door swing.
  • What product type you are trying to buy.

What helps most

Specific measurements are easier to turn into useful guides. Mention pipes, outlets, baseboards, handles, trim, lids, drawers, and anything that needs room to move.
