Quick verdict

For an under sink organizer around pipes, choose adjustable shelves, split two-tier organizers, or removable caddies before fixed-width drawers. Anything with a solid back is more likely to hit plumbing.

Measure this before buying

  • Interior cabinet width, depth, and height.
  • Pipe location from left wall, right wall, back wall, and cabinet floor.
  • Clear space in front of the P-trap, disposal, filter, or valves.
  • Door opening width after hinges and center stiles.

Common fit mistakes

  • Measuring the cabinet but not the door opening.
  • Forgetting valves that need to stay reachable.
  • Buying a pull-out drawer before checking the full slide path.
  • Covering the cabinet floor so leaks are harder to notice.

FAQ

What type of under-sink organizer works around pipes?

Adjustable shelves, split organizers, caddies, and separate bins usually handle plumbing better than solid-back drawers.

Should I use a pull-out organizer under the sink?

Only if the drawer can slide forward without hitting pipes, valves, disposal, or cabinet hinges.

How do I measure under a sink for organizers?

Measure the cabinet interior, door opening, and pipe position from the sides, back, and floor.
